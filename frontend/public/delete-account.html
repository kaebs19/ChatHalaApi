ملاحظة عن الاشتراك

حذف الحساب لا يُلغي اشتراك Google Play. ألغِه من تطبيق Google Play ← الاشتراكات ← دردشاتي.

Delete your Dardashati account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete account.

By email: write to kaebs19@gmail.com from the address registered on your account. We verify ownership and delete within 30 days.

Deleted: profile details, photos, messages, likes, device tokens — permanently. Reports filed against the account are kept, stripped of your identity, to protect other users. Backups rotate within 30 days.

Note: deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.
